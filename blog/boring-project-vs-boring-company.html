We're Not Elon Musk's Boring Company. But We Get Why You're Confused.

By Joseph Gutierrez · August 2026 · 3 min read

No affiliation with The Boring Company, Elon Musk, or Tesla. Just two businesses that happened to pick the same word.

If you searched "boring project san antonio" and landed on a page about underground tunnels and Tesla-powered transit loops — you're not lost. You're not losing it. You just ran into the most confusing coincidence in San Antonio branding right now.

Here's the deal: boring project. is a local web design and SEO studio, built for San Antonio service businesses. The Boring Company is Elon Musk's tunnel-digging company, currently in talks with the city about a pedestrian tunnel for Morgan's Wonderland. Same word in the name. Completely different businesses. Zero relation.

Honestly, we didn't see this coming when we picked the name.

Wait, Elon Musk is actually doing something in San Antonio?

Yeah, really. The Boring Company has been in and out of San Antonio conversations for a few years now — there was an airport-to-downtown tunnel proposal that didn't move forward, and more recently, San Antonio made it into the finalist round of TBC's national "Tunnel Vision Challenge." The current live project is a proposed pedestrian tunnel tied to Morgan's Wonderland, and as of this year, it's reportedly moved into a funded diligence phase. Whether it actually gets built is still an open question — TBC has a long list of announced projects across the country that never made it past the proposal stage.

So if you searched "boring company san antonio" hoping to find out what's actually happening with that — that's a real, current story, and worth following. It's just not us.

Okay, so why did we pick the same word?

Because we mean the opposite thing by it.

Elon Musk picked "boring" because tunneling is, technically, boring — it's slow, unglamorous engineering work that nobody thinks about until they're stuck in traffic wishing it existed.

We picked "boring" because of the businesses we work with. The HVAC company that shows up in August when your AC dies. The plumber who fixes the leak before it floods your kitchen. The demolition crew, the roofer, the landscaper. Boring, in the sense that nobody's making a Netflix documentary about them. Also boring in the sense that they are the actual infrastructure of this city running underneath everything, the same way Elon Musk's underneath everything is, well, underneath everything.

We just build websites instead of tunnels.

The businesses we call "boring" are the least boring thing about San Antonio

Here's the thing that started boring project. in the first place: these businesses are excellent at what they do and almost invisible online. Not because they're bad at business — because nobody built anything affordable, fast, or local for them. Traditional agencies want $5,000–$10,000 upfront and a year-long contract. DIY website builders produce something generic that looks like every other DIY site. Freelancers build it and disappear.

So we built something different — websites and local SEO management built specifically for San Antonio service businesses, priced for what they actually make, live in days instead of months, and managed every month instead of abandoned after launch.

Nobody's digging a tunnel under 281. But somebody's making sure the plumber who could fix your emergency at 2am actually shows up on page one of Google when you search for one. That felt worth building.

So, to recap:

✓ Looking for Elon Musk's tunnel company? That's The Boring Company — boringcompany.com.
✓ Looking for a website and local SEO for your San Antonio service business? That's us — boring project. — and you're already in the right place.

Same word. Different mission. One of us is underground. The other one's trying to get your business found above it.
